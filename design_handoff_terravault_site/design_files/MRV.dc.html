Every ton is measured, reported, and verified — never modeled

Our measurement, reporting, and verification protocol is the reason a Terravault ton survives diligence. This is how a molecule of CO₂ becomes a credit you can defend.

Continuous flow metering · Facility 02
The measurement chain

Three meters, one reconciled number

CO₂ is metered at every point it moves. Nothing is estimated between them.

M1

Capture meter

Mass-flow sensors log concentrated CO₂ leaving each contactor in real time, calibrated weekly against a reference gas.

M2

Injection meter

A second independent meter records exactly what enters the wellhead for mineralization — the ton that actually goes underground.

M3

Reconciliation

Capture and injection totals are reconciled hourly. Any variance beyond 0.5% flags the batch for review before a credit is issued.
