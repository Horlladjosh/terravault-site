Photography & handoff

01
Real facility photography, treated

Industrial and landscape imagery over stock people. Always apply the dark gradient overlay (bottom 180deg to #090b09/92%, plus a left 90deg scrim) for legible captions and live-data tags. Never flat color blocks. Avoid hard-hat-and-smile stock.

02
Swap accent to reflex the vertical

The system flexes to battery storage and low-carbon data centers by changing only --accent and its gradient stops. Keep the near-black canvas, type pairing, and spacing fixed across verticals.

03
Framer build notes

Set the tokens above as Framer color and text styles. Container 1160px, gutter 64px (24px on mobile). Build cards, buttons, badges, stat cells, and the FAQ as components. Scroll reveal via Framer's appear animation using the reveal easing. Accent stays scarce — CTAs and hero numbers only.
